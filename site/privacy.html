Rating privacy

The review build keeps rating selections and a random voter token in your browser. A configured production rating service will receive that token, the selected game version and your star ratings. No name, email or free-text review is requested.

The service uses the token to replace your earlier rating for the same game version. This is best-effort anonymous feedback; clearing storage or changing browsers can create another token. It does not verify one person per vote.

When enabled, aggregate averages and counts are public; individual vote tokens are not published. Hosting providers may process technical request logs, including IP addresses, under their own policies. The application does not intentionally store IP addresses in the rating database.

No AI model or API key is needed to play. This page's public rating service is not yet deployed. Hosting and retention details must be updated when production collection is enabled.
